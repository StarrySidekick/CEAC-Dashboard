About

As New York and Westchester advance a clean, affordable and reliable energy future, battery energy storage systems are playing an increasingly important role. Many localities are working through the technical, legal, planning and public safety questions that come with them. This free half-day conference gives municipal leaders and public safety professionals the latest information, practical guidance and best practices to support informed local decision-making.
