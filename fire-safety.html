Energy Storage Fire & Safety

As battery energy storage systems (BESS) and other clean technologies become more common, public concern over fire risk has grown, often fueled by misunderstanding or outdated data. Here is a clear, evidence-based look at the record.

BESS and other renewable systems are significantly safer than our fossil fuel energy systems of pumps, pipelines, refineries, plants, and residential and commercial structures. The widespread adoption of BESS directly reduces the need to rely on those systems, and provides many additional practical benefits.

Clean energy systems require a source of generation (wind, solar, hydropower), a place to store that energy (BESS), and distribution infrastructure. Fossil fuel systems require a more complex and messy logistics pipeline of extraction, refinement, and transport, which leaves multiple vulnerabilities to fire and other accidents.

The rules that changed
UL 954011 and UL 9540A12 — national energy storage safety standards that add rigorous protocols to new BESS systems.
NFPA 85513 — the standard for installation of stationary energy storage systems.
Model Energy Storage Ordinance Framework14 — released June 2025 by the American Clean Power Association, addressing issues present in older energy storage systems.
Real-time Battery Management Systems15 — catch thermal runaway events 50 to 80 cycles before they occur.
NYSERDA Quality Assurance15 — 57 in-field inspections of BESS facilities over 300 kW, leading to enhanced facility oversight, inspection process, and code recommendations.
Anatomy of an incident

Two New York events, side by side

One caused by fossil fuels, the other by energy storage.

2014 · Fossil fuel

East Harlem Gas Explosion16

  • Caused by a leaking natural gas pipeline under a residential street
  • Explosion destroyed two apartment buildings instantly
  • 8 fatalities, 50+ injuries, many displaced
  • Fires raged for hours, with widespread debris and property damage
  • Actual steps to future prevention remain murky and currently unknown
2023 · Energy storage

East Hampton BESS Failure17

  • Triggered by an overheating module in a utility-scale lithium-ion battery system
  • Automatic safety systems detected the issue and initiated safe shutdown
  • No deaths or injuries, minimal damage
  • Contained to the storage unit, with no explosive fuel or fire spread
  • Prompted direct and effective legislation and regulation preventing recurrence
This comparison underscores a critical point: while fossil fuel accidents often produce catastrophic explosions, fatalities, and widespread damage, modern BESS systems are designed with containment and fail-safes that minimize risk.
What stops fires in a BESS

Fire Prevention Systems18

Layers that detect a problem early and shut the affected equipment down before a fire can develop.

Battery Management Systems

Keep cells within safe temperature ranges.

Voltage & temperature sensors

Detect abnormal conditions early.

Automatic disconnects & fuses

Isolate failing cells or racks.

Metal separators & flame-retardant cell fill

Slow heat and fire spread.

Automated shutdown protocols

Activate before a fire develops.

Remote monitoring & control

Allows human operators to depower or isolate the system.

Smoke, heat & gas detectors

Identify incipient thermal runaway.

Pre-planned emergency procedures

Coordinated with local fire departments.

Fire Suppression Systems18

If an event does begin, these contain it to the unit where it started.

Steel & fire-rated enclosures

Surround the entire system.

Fire gaps & firewalls

Contain runaway events.

Clean-agent gas

Releases inert gas to extinguish fires.

Water mist & sprinkler deluge

Releases water and mist.

Pressure relief vents

Safely release any build-up of gases.

Aerosol or dry-chemical agents

The material found in handheld fire extinguishers.

The bottom line

Battery energy storage systems and other clean energy technologies operate under fundamentally different safety assumptions than fossil fuel systems. Despite being newer technology, modern BESS units are already rapidly growing safer year by year, and regulations with protocols are now in place to stop incidents.

Early detection, containment, and automated shutdowns that isolate incidents before they can escalate are now the standard.

BESS is a safe, reliable, and lasting energy storage option.
