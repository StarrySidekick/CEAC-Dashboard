Local codes change, and public notices are easy to miss. If something in the Live Policy Tracker is out of date or wrong, tell us here and it goes straight to the coalition.

Thank you — your note is on its way. We read every submission and check it against the town’s code and minutes before the tracker changes. If you left an email address we may follow up for the source.

Every field except the description is optional, but the more you can give us, the faster we can verify and publish the change. A link to the adopted local law, the meeting minutes, or the legal notice is the single most useful thing you can include.

Submissions reach the coalition by email through FormSubmit, a third-party relay. Nothing you enter is published on this site, and nothing changes in the tracker until a person has checked it against the source.
